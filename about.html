ABOUT US

A council run by engineers, for engineers.

The Technical Council is IIT Dharwad's student body for everything technical outside the classroom — workshops, hackathons, open-source projects and the speaker series that connects students to people already building. How we work

01

Propose

Anyone can pitch a workshop, project or event to the council — no seniority required.

02

Team up

Domain leads match volunteers to the proposal and set a two-week build cadence.

03

Ship

Every initiative gets a real deadline and a real audience — a talk, a demo day, a release.

04

Share

Write-ups and repos go public so the next batch of students can build on them.

How the council is structured

A general secretary and a junior general secretary set direction; nine student clubs do the building.

What we care about

Build in public

Repos, write-ups and post-mortems stay open by default.

Mentorship over hierarchy

Second-years lead sessions for first-years within a month of joining.

Ship, don't just plan

Every proposal carries a deadline and a demo, not just a deck.

Open to every branch

No CS prerequisite — half the council isn't from Comps or Math & Computing.
